## Build trustworthy history from now on
Do not rewrite or backdate history. Open an issue with acceptance criteria, create a branch, make one coherent change, add tests, run lint/tests, commit, record limitations, and seek review. Suggested commit style: `test(tenancy): cover cross-organization report access`.

## No false claims
Do not claim eight months of development without dated evidence; eight months of NIC experience based on this independent project; real customers, revenue, adoption, or production traffic without evidence; sole authorship of generated/copied work; or external validation where only self-review occurred. This project cannot retroactively prove employment, internship, customer outcomes, or elapsed duration.
